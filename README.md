## B.Sc. BIOLOGY
I concentrated my major studied in the area of **Ecology and Evolutionary biology**; I also obtained a minor in **Computer Information Systems**.
During my undergraduate degree I assisted with research on the topics of maladaptation and local adaptation with [Jon Mee](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-0688-1390) and [Sam Yeaman](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-1706-8699); the paper resulting from our research is published in the journal of [The American Naturalist](https://doi.org/10.1086/730186); [bioRxiv hosts the freely available preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.03.16.532996).
